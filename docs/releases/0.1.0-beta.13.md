# Bloblex 0.1.0-beta.13

## Changes

### Blobs work as a team
- Projects come first in the sidebar. Blobs are grouped by project, with a Pinned section and Unassigned for casual blobs, and can be dragged between groups. Each blob has one conversation.
- Blobs have a role label next to their name, and one blob can lead the team (an orange star on its avatar).
- A blob can message a teammate. Tag one with `@Name`, or ask the leader to hand work over. The leader opens a side conversation ("A ⇄ B"), sends a brief and tells you it is waiting. When the teammate replies, the leader is woken up and reports back with its own verdict and any open questions. "Messaged X" and "Message from X" open the side conversation, and `@` mentions show the blob's face and name in its colour.
- Every blob knows it runs inside Bloblex with teammates: its name, role, project, what it is for, and your profile name (or "admin"). Teammates are reached through an app-owned MCP server.
- Right-click a blob for Pin, Move to, Mark as unread, Rename, Make team leader, Edit, Duplicate, Export, Copy conversation ID, Delete conversation, Hide from sidebar and Archive.

### Plan mode and questions
- A Plan switch in the composer. A proposed plan appears as a card with Approve & build or Keep planning.
- Questions from Claude Code and Codex appear in the conversation as lettered options, with your own answer as a fallback.

### Composer
- Model, effort, fast mode and permissions controls, a context ring, and image attachments for Claude Code and Codex.
- A conversation outline rail, and a borderless Full access indicator.

### Blob looks
- Outfits are replaced by ten shapes: Round, Organic, Boxy, Capsule, Cloud, Droplet, Hexagon, Sun, Triangle and Cat. They are adapted from blobatar (MIT); the Cat is Bloblex's own.
- Every shape keeps the full character: blinking, gaze, squash, sleeping, the file-drop mailbox and the badges. "Done" rolls backwards, and boxy, hexagon, sun and triangle spin instead.
- A new Look editor with a live stage that previews moods, shape and colour tiles, a custom colour swatch, and eye fine-tuning. Bodies share one footprint.
- The round mascot leads onboarding, the launch screen and the welcome wave.

### Companion
- The companion is a mini version of the app. It shows the focused blob with its role, leader star and "Waiting on X" line, beside the whole team grouped by project with live waiting and working states.
- Its chat shows team chips, `@` mentions and plans. Questions can be answered from the island, and Activity lists side conversations.
- New original synthesized sounds for the companion's moments: peeking in, opening, closing, switching, sending, a new reply, pokes, work, finish, error, approval and question. They are louder than before, unlock on your first click, and still play with reduced motion on.

### Launch, import and settings
- Startup checks scroll in a fixed window that fades into the background. The mascot works while Bloblex scans, then waves hello before the app opens; click to skip.
- Importing a blob previews it in its own shape and colour, lists what comes with it, and lets you pick the coding agent from cards.
- The plan usage bar along the bottom is now an Experimental setting (Settings → General → Experimental), off by default.

### Fixes
- The import dialog and the bypass-approval confirmation now appear. They previously opened invisible.
- The archive card no longer shows a stray divider line.

## Verification

- All 366 desktop tests, typecheck, and the production web build passed.
- The offline Rust workspace suite passed, including a storage test for teams and looks and a daemon test of the full leader → teammate → relayed-reply flow against a fake provider. The desktop crate compiled in the release bundle build.
- Live checks with isolated databases. Codex leader → OpenCode teammate delegation passed 8/8, and the reverse pairing passed 8/8. Codex plan mode with a structured question passed 6/6.
- The new screens were checked in the browser preview, including `/gallery.html`.
- Not verified: Claude Code live delegation and plan mode (its CLI sign-in had expired), real sound output, native window behaviour, and clean-machine installation.
