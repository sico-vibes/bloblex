# Bitbots 0.1.0-beta.30

The owner's review of beta.29, worked through: a faster app after the first load, many
conversations per bot, a shared pool of skills, backup and restore, and Remote over Tailscale.
**Remote is new and has not been tested against a live tailnet or a real phone yet; read
"Remote" before turning it on.**

## New

- **Feels instant after it loads.** Models and effort levels are cached at boot and kept fresh in
  the background, so switching them never waits. Sending never locks the composer: the message
  shows at once as "Sending…" and the coding agent starts behind it ("Starting Codex…"). The open
  conversation's coding agent is warmed when you select it, conversations load in the
  background (the selected bot's first), and dictation is warmed at boot and when the window
  comes back.
- **Many conversations per bot.** Clicking a bot opens a Conversations column (collapsible, like
  the right panel) with every conversation in its project, auto-titled from the first message,
  newest first, with New, Rename, Archive and Delete. Bot-to-bot conversations hang under the
  conversation that started them, with both bots' avatars, and expand or collapse.
- **Session rail.** The right panel is now four cards: Environment (computer, project folder,
  git branch, coding agent, Remote), Context (how full, with the token split), Outputs (files
  the conversation created) and Changes (every changed file with its diff).
- **Latest changes banner.** After a turn that changed files, a strip at the top of the
  conversation lists them with their line counts, the diff and Undo. A second line suggests a
  new conversation when the context is over 80% full.
- **Skills.** One pool of skills shared by every bot (Settings › Skills): import the skills
  Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode already have, or any folder with a `SKILL.md`. Bots see a short
  list and load a skill with the new `load_skill` tool when a task matches; a skill your coding
  agent already loads itself is not loaded twice. Turn single skills off for one bot on its page.
  A skill load shows as "Used skill: name" in the turn's steps.
- **Backup.** Settings › Backup (also in the profile menu) saves your bots, their memory, skills,
  automations and app settings to one checked file, and restores it after showing what it holds
  and asking about every name conflict. Conversations, projects, sign-ins and secrets are never
  included; automations come back paused.
- **Remote over Tailscale.** See below.

## Remote

Remote lets your phone, a tablet or another computer on **your own Tailscale network** use your
bots. Bitbots runs a small gateway on this computer (loopback only) and publishes it with
`tailscale serve`; it never uses Tailscale Funnel and never opens a port to your network.

- Turn it on in Settings › Remote. If Tailscale is missing, signed out, or HTTPS certificates are
  off for your tailnet, the page says exactly what to fix.
- Choose "Show pairing code", then open the address on the phone (or scan the code) and type the
  code. The computer asks you to approve the device and choose what it may do: View only, Chat,
  or Chat and approve (approving from a device is off until you allow it).
- The phone app (installable from the browser) lists your bots and their conversations and
  chats within the device's permissions. It needs the computer to be reachable; nothing from
  your conversations is stored on the phone.
- Devices can be renamed, downgraded or removed at any time; removing one signs it out at once.

**Not verified yet:** a live tailnet, a real phone, iOS Safari's cookie handling, the installed
web app, and the 443 → 8443 fallback. The gateway's behaviour is covered by 75 tests against a
simulated Tailscale. The owner accepted the Remote rules in `AGENTS.md` on 17 October 2026 and
chose to ship it in this beta before the planned threat-model checklist and independent review.

## Changed

- "?" tooltips replace small print under settings, in the bot editor and in the companion.
- The model picker has a search field; effort levels have icons and no sub-text.
- "Messaged" and "Message from" chips open the bot-to-bot conversation; a "Created bot" notice
  links to the new bot.
- Onboarding's first-bot step lets you set the look, name, role and description.
- The bot editor is one centred column; permission cards have icons; the save bar drops the
  shortcut hint. Message actions are back to copy, quote and react in a row.
- The sidebar usage meter rotates between providers; signed-out providers say "Sign-in needed".
- "Bot" is used everywhere, in both looks.
- Companion: drag from any empty space again, roomier peer chips, a full-width composer laid out
  like the main one, and bot-to-bot conversations open inside the companion.
- Plan mode and the permission mode change at once and roll back if the change fails.

## Manual installation

Download `Bitbots_0.1.0-beta.30_x64-setup.exe` and verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The
installer is unsigned (Windows may warn). No updater manifest is published; the signed
`channel-beta` feed remains at beta.6. Install beta.30 manually.
