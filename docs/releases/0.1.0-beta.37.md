# Bitbots 0.1.0-beta.37

A new primary look, **Haze**, and the Tasks board. If you run beta.32 or later, this version downloads by itself and shows **Restart to update** in the top bar.

## New

- **Haze, the new primary look.** Smoky glass in one calm window, with no mascots. Each bot shows its coding agent's logo (in its own colours, in the bot's colour, or in one colour) or one of 16 pixel icons in the bot's colour. Pick it in the bot's settings under Look. A bot's status is a soft gradient that rolls in from the right of its row: slow and muted when it is selected, livelier while it thinks and works, amber when it needs you, red when it fails, green for a moment when it finishes. It shows only while a bot is busy or selected, and it fades between states. The Manager keeps a still golden fade. New installs start in Haze; if you already chose a look, it stays.
- **A new companion in Haze.** The companion is now a small copy of the main window: your bots on the left, each with its status gradient, and the open conversation on the right, with approvals, activity and settings in the same place. Collapsed, it is one bar with the focused bot and its busiest teammates. You can still drag it anywhere.
- **Tasks.** A Tasks page (the button in the sidebar header) with a board: drafts on the left and a lane per bot on the right. Drag a draft onto a bot, or use its Assign menu, to start it or queue it. A tree shows how the Manager split the work, and a timeline shows each bot's runs. The Manager can draft tasks for you to hand out; only you assign them.
- **Archived bots and permanent deletion.** Archived bots have their own view in the profile menu, and a bot can be deleted for good with a press-and-hold confirmation.

## Changed

- **Three looks, with new names.** The picker offers Haze, Frost and Stone. Frost is the look called Bitbots until now, and Stone is the look called Blobots; your choice carries over unchanged. Pop, Dot and Legacy are removed; if you used one of them, Bitbots opens in Frost. The look orbs are redrawn as one set.
- **Usage rings** are now an outer ring with the coding agent's logo in the middle.
- An update notice sits above your profile in the sidebar, and settings pages show placeholders while they load.

## Fixed

- Links in chat open in your browser, and bare GitHub links are formatted.
- Pasted images, the side-conversation header, mention contrast, profile menu dividers and finished task rows.

## Installation

Running beta.32 or later: wait for **Restart to update**, or check now in Settings › Updates. Older versions: download `Bitbots_0.1.0-beta.37_x64-setup.exe` and verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The installer is not Authenticode-signed (Windows may warn).
