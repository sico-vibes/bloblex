# Bitbots 0.1.0-beta.31

The owner's review of beta.30, worked through: the fixes and polish from the screenshots, a
second way to reach your bots from your phone without Tailscale, and the **Manager**, one bot
that runs your team and asks before it acts. **Direct remote access is new and has not been
tested from a real phone or router yet; read "Remote without Tailscale" before turning it on.**

## New

- **The Manager.** One permanent bot pinned above every project. It knows every project and
  bot, plans the work with you, and proposes projects, bots and tasks as cards in the chat that
  you can edit, approve or reject. Nothing is created until you approve. You can rename it,
  change its look, model and personality; it can't be deleted. New installs meet it in
  onboarding; existing installs get a one-time "Meet your Manager" card in the sidebar, and the
  old team leader becomes an ordinary bot.
- **What the Manager knows.** It keeps notes about you (how you work, your conventions, your
  taste) and a playbook for how it interviews you and plans a team. Both stay when you change
  its model, so the next model picks up where the last one left off ("New model, same
  Manager"). Settings › Manager lists the notes, where you can edit, delete or lock them (a locked
  note needs your approval to change), and the playbook with its history, what each version
  changed, and Restore.
- **Start a project with the Manager.** The menu next to "+" in the sidebar has "New project
  with the Manager" and "Import project folder…", which open its conversation with a message
  ready to edit and send.
- **Questions for every coding agent.** Any bot can ask you a short questionnaire in the chat
  (options, "other", several answers), not only OpenCode.
- **Remote without Tailscale ("Direct").** See below.
- **What's new.** This page, in the profile menu, and shown once after an update.

## Remote without Tailscale

Settings › Remote now offers two ways in, one at a time: **Tailscale** (as before) or
**Direct**, which needs only Bitbots.

- You set a username and password. A device opens this computer's address, signs in, and then
  waits for you to approve it here and choose what it may do, as with Tailscale.
- Bitbots serves HTTPS with its own certificate. The browser warns once per device; compare the
  fingerprint it shows with the one in Settings › Remote before continuing.
- "Reaching this computer from away" walks through the rest: ask your router to forward the
  port (only if you allow it, and Bitbots removes the forward when Remote turns off), allow the
  port through Windows Firewall (Windows asks you to confirm), check the public address (it
  warns when your provider shares one address between customers, which Direct can't get
  through), and test from your phone on mobile data.
- Wrong passwords are slowed down, then locked out per address, and sign-in pauses for everyone
  after a burst. Bitbots contacts no outside service to find your address.

**Not verified yet:** a real phone on mobile data, a real router's port forwarding, the Windows
Firewall prompt, and the certificate's file permissions on a fresh install. All of this is
covered only by tests against simulated routers and firewalls. The owner approved Direct in
`AGENTS.md` on 17 October 2026; the independent security review is still owed before a
non-beta release.

## Fixed

- The phone web app loads instead of saying "Bitbots Remote is not installed".
- "Started a fresh session" no longer repeats in conversations that hadn't started yet.
- An empty "New chat" isn't kept, and a bot that already has a project opens straight into a
  composer instead of "Choose a project".
- An expired coding-agent sign-in is flagged on the bot and in the error card, with **Sign in
  again** (opens a terminal running the agent's own sign-in) and **Install** for a missing one.
- A pending approval no longer stops you from opening another bot while it waits.

## Changed

- The header reads "Project / Conversation", with the conversations button at the far left.
- The Remote chip is a small sidebar banner with the method and a status light.
- The selected bot is clearly highlighted in light mode, and only the selected bot's eyes
  follow the pointer.
- The profile menu is grouped, with What's new and the tour.
- The command palette groups results by kind, with icons and shortcuts.
- Conversation rows hide the time under their buttons, and branches have tree lines.
- The companion can switch conversations and shows the bot's project.

## Manual installation

Download `Bitbots_0.1.0-beta.31_x64-setup.exe` and verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The
installer is unsigned (Windows may warn). No updater manifest is published; the signed
`channel-beta` feed remains at beta.6. Install beta.31 manually.
