## UI: `apps/desktop/src`

- Entry: `main.tsx` imports every stylesheet as static imports, including the look stylesheet `ui/themes/haze.css` (Frost's was deleted on 27 October 2026); never load a look stylesheet with a dynamic `import()` (lesson FIX42; `ui/themeBuild.test.ts` enforces it). `blob/bitbots.ts` normalizes retired cached themes to Haze. `index.html` has a tiny pre-paint script that sets `data-theme`, `data-style` and friends from localStorage.
