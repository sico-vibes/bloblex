# Bitbots 0.1.0-beta.35

The look wave. **Bitbots** is the new primary look: flat translucent panels over a wallpaper.
Pop and Dot are two new looks, and the old pixel look is kept as Legacy. Settings › Appearance
is rebuilt, bots can choose their own art, and the companion and dictation windows follow the
look. Links open in your browser, you can paste images into the composer, and there is an
update notice for the command line tools. If you run beta.32, beta.33 or beta.34, this version
downloads by itself and shows **Restart to update** in the top bar.

## New

- **Bitbots is the new primary look.** It is Frost: flat translucent panels over a wallpaper.
  Choose the wallpaper (Lake by default, or Dusk, Bloom, Sand, or your own PNG, JPEG or WebP
  image up to 15 MB) and the glass level (**Solid** by default, **Balanced** or **Clear**).
  Existing Bitbots users get Frost automatically.
- **Pop and Dot.** Pop has retro colour and outlined cards. Dot is monochrome, with dot-matrix
  numbers and one red accent. Both use rounded rectangles only. Blobots stays the alternative look.
- **Legacy.** The previous pixel look, under **More looks**. Switch to it in Settings › Appearance.
- **Settings › Appearance is rebuilt.** An orb for each look, then look options (wallpaper and
  glass), colour (theme and accent), and text (interface font, code font, text size and density).
  There are 24 bundled fonts, including pixel fonts.
- **Onboarding** offers Bitbots and Blobots, with Legacy, Pop and Dot under **More looks**.
- **Bot styles.** In each bot's editor, choose 3D robot, Flat robot, Pop robot, Dot robot,
  Glossy blob or Hand-painted (twelve shapes). A bot without a choice follows the look. The new
  robot styles are much lighter than the 3D robot. Hand-painted bots show their status in a
  small corner badge.
- **The companion and dictation windows follow the look.** Frost, Pop and Dot style both
  windows in light and dark. Frost uses a solid tint there, because these windows cannot blur
  what is behind them. Legacy and Blobots look as before.
- **An update notice for the command line tools.** Bitbots shows a notice for the Claude Code,
  Codex and OpenCode command line tools.
- **Paste images into the composer** from the clipboard.
- **Clickable links** in chat and in the companion.

## Changed

- **Launch greeting.** Legacy keeps the pixel welcome, and the glossy blob keeps its classic
  greeting. Every other look shows the bot in the art it wears.
- **Companion styling.** Legacy and Blobots companion styling is unchanged.

## Fixed

- **Links.** Middle-click, and Ctrl, Cmd or Shift-click, could make the app open or navigate a
  link on its own. Every way of clicking a link now goes through the safe opener and opens your
  browser. If the opener fails inside the app, nothing else opens. Dragging a link no longer
  drops its address into the app.
- **"This look has no extra options yet."** The line sat flush against the card edge in Pop
  and the other looks. It now lines up with the other rows.

## Verification

- Desktop UI (Vitest): 1,352 tests in 180 files passed. `npx tsc -b` and `git diff --check`
  are clean.
- Companion and dictation text in the browser preview: every text element measured at least
  4.5:1 against its background, for Frost, Pop and Dot, in dark and light.
- Legacy and Blobots companion styling: a fingerprint of 17 properties on every visible
  element is identical before and after the change.
- Rust gates on the merged release source (`f182e63`): the full workspace excluding the
  desktop crate passed, `bloblex-daemon` 456, `bloblex-storage` 119 and the desktop library 63,
  and the production web build succeeded. Clippy is clean after a lint fix in `bloblex-storage`.

Unverified: the new looks were checked in the browser preview, not in an installed build. Not
yet checked: the transparent companion and dictation windows over other apps; the wallpaper
picker through the native file dialog; the Geist font in the native window (the face did not

the remote (phone) web app still shows robots by default; the new bot styles in live provider
sessions, and the Manager with a live model; the main-window looks, which were checked only in
the preview and the tests; and the update to this version and a clean install on a real machine.

## Installation

Running beta.32, beta.33 or beta.34: wait for **Restart to update**, or check now in
Settings › Updates. Older versions: download `Bitbots_0.1.0-beta.35_x64-setup.exe` and verify
it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The installer is not Authenticode-signed (Windows may warn).
