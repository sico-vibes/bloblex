# Bitbots 0.1.0-beta.47

This release is a facelift: fewer tabs, less text, and nothing moving or glowing without a reason. If you run beta.32 or later, this version downloads by itself.

## Added

- **Chat background.** In Haze, choose an image in Settings › Appearance and pick an effect: None, Dither, ASCII, Halftone, Scanlines or Haze. You choose whether it shows on empty chats only or on every conversation, and how strong it is on each. It's off until you choose an image, and the image stays on this computer.
- **Project icons.** Pick an emoji or a preset icon in a colour from a project's menu. With no pick, a project shows the favicon from its own folder if it has one, otherwise a folder.
- **Blurred emails.** An account's email stays blurred until you click it, for screenshots and screen shares.

## Changed

- **Settings.** 17 tabs are now 10, in four groups. The Experimental tab is gone, and each switch moved to the tab it belongs on. Your choices are kept. Automations are now called Schedules everywhere.
- **Usage.** One panel shows every limit at once, from the bottom bar or the sidebar, and each meter marks how much of its window has passed. The Usage page is rebuilt with meters, and its Tokens, Run time and Runs tiles choose what the chart shows.
- **Haze follows your accent.** With an accent, the background takes its colour. With none, it's a neutral grey, not orange.
- **Haze's controls.** Buttons, toggles, segmented controls and sliders are squarer and flatter. Stone keeps its own.
- **Messages.** Copy, reply, react and the time now sit under a message, so a long reply needs no scrolling back up.
- **Needs you.** It's now a bell beside the buttons at the top of the sidebar, with a count and a list.
- **Composer.** Plan moved into the mode menu with the permission modes. Steer and Queue left the composer: the choice is in Settings › Chat, and Ctrl+Enter still sends the other way.
- **Dragging a task.** The card itself now moves, and it flies back if you drop it away from a bot.
- **Right panel.** The sections are flat, empty ones are hidden, and Changes has tidier columns.
- **Status.** A bot's state is now a word at the end of its row. The moving gradient and the coloured outlines on rows, chips, approvals and plans are gone.
- **Keyboard shortcuts.** They all share one look. To clear a shortcut, press Backspace while recording it.
- **Less text.** Long page introductions, explanations and repeated labels are shorter or gone.

## Fixed

- A search box could show two focus outlines at once.
- A schedule's next run could read "19 h 60 min".
- A Codex turn that finished very quickly could still count as running, so a later Codex crash marked it as failed.

## Not yet checked on real hardware

Tested in the browser preview, with real Rust tests for reading a project's favicon. **Not tried for real:** these screens inside the installed app, a favicon from a real project on screen, a chat background surviving a restart, and how Windows' frosted glass looks under an accent colour. Applying a background effect can take up to about 140 ms on a large image.

## Installation

Running beta.32 or later: wait for **Restart to update**, or check now in Settings › Updates. Older versions: download `Bitbots_0.1.0-beta.47_x64-setup.exe` and verify it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
